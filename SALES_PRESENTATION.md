# School Routine Coordinator — Sales Presentation Deck

*Slide-by-slide script for a 20+ slide PowerPoint / Google Slides presentation to school decision-makers (principal, vice-principal, administration, trustees).*

**How to use this file:** Each block below is one slide. Copy the Bullets into your slide, and read the **Speaker Note** aloud. The deck follows a persuasive arc: Hook → The problem → The solution → The smart engine → Reports → Reliability → Time/money → Close.

## Slide 3 — The Traditional Way: A Week of Pain

**On screen:**
- 1–2 weeks spent drafting the master timetable by hand
- Manual cross-checking for double-booked teachers & rooms
- Photocopied routines, outdated by the first change
- Substitutions arranged over the phone, guessed
- No record of the day's substitutions
- "What's running right now?" — walk to the board

**Speaker note:**
> "Let's be honest about what the manual process really costs. It takes one to two weeks to draft the master timetable, and it's usually done by one senior teacher. Conflicts slip through — a teacher ends up in two rooms, or two classes in one. The printed routine is outdated by lunchtime. And when a teacher is absent, someone is on the phone guessing who's free. At the end of the day there's no record of what happened."

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution at a Glance

**On screen:**
- A live digital weekly timetable for every section
- Conflict prevention built into the database
- Daily substitutions in under a minute
- Published instantly to staff, students & parents
- Live "current period" display
- One-click branded PDF reports

**Speaker note:**
> "The School Routine Coordinator fixes all of this. It's a digital timetable for every class and section, it prevents conflicts automatically, it handles daily substitutions in under a minute, it publishes instantly, and it produces real reports. Let's see the pieces."

---

## Slide 5 — Master Data: One Source of Truth

**On screen bullets:**
- Classes, Sections, Rooms, Subjects, Teachers, Admins — all in one place
- Section → fixed room, so rooms are never lost
- Teacher → primary subject + subjects taught + "open teacher" flag
- Update once; every view stays consistent

**Vs. traditional:**
- No more correcting the same name in five registers

**Speaker note:**
> "Everything starts with clean data. The system keeps one registry of classes, sections, rooms, subjects and teachers. Each section is tied to a fixed room, and each teacher is linked to the subjects they can actually teach. You update a name or a room once, and it propagates everywhere automatically."

---

## Slide 6 — The Routine Builder: Draft in Hours, Not Weeks

**On screen bullets:**
- Interactive 5-day × 7-period grid per section
- Click a cell → pick subject, teacher, room
- Teachers auto-filtered to subjects they teach
- Drag-and-drop to swap periods
- Two-teacher "tag" sessions supported
- Room auto-fills from the section; overrides preserved

**Vs. traditional:**
- Weeks → hours; errors prevented before they happen

**Speaker note:**
> "This is where the time disappears. Instead of crossing out conflicts on paper, the coordinator clicks a cell and picks a subject, teacher and room. The system only shows teachers who can actually teach that subject, so many mistakes are impossible. You can drag periods to swap them, set up two-teacher sessions, and the room fills in automatically from the section. A term's timetable goes from weeks to hours."

---

## Slide 7 — The Smart Engine: Conflicts That Never Happen

**On screen bullets:**
- A teacher can NEVER be double-booked at the same day & period — enforced by the database
- One primary session per period cell
- Substitutes checked for availability automatically
- Workload warnings: 5+ periods/day (amber), 6+ (red); 3+ continuous (amber), 4+ (red)
- Lunch break correctly resets the "continuous periods" counter

**Vs. traditional:**
- The clashes that caused corridor chaos are now prevented

**Speaker note:**
> "Here's the heart of the product. The system doesn't just warn about conflicts — it prevents them at the database level. A teacher physically cannot be assigned to two sections at the same time. Beyond that, it watches workload: five or more periods in a day, or three or more back-to-back, gets flagged, and the lunch break is handled correctly. The human errors that caused daily chaos are now impossible."

I can add a two-teacher session here. And if I try to put a teacher in two places at once, the system blocks it — it simply won't save. This is what I mean by 'conflicts never happen.'"

## Slide 9 — Daily Substitutions: Under a Minute

**On screen bullets:**
- Pick the date (weekends blocked)
- Pick the absent teacher — see their whole day with colour-coded load
- Click a period → ranked list of substitutes, free-first
- Live stats: classes today, continuous stretch, weekly total
- "Already 5 classes today" / "4 continuous" warnings
- Busy teachers shown dimmed; available highlighted

**Vs. traditional:**
- Phone calls & guesswork → one ranked screen

**Speaker note:**
> "When a teacher is absent, this is the workflow. Pick the date, pick the absent teacher, and you see their full day. Click the period you need to cover, and the system shows every available teacher, sorted free-first, with their daily load, their continuous stretch, and their weekly total — with warnings if someone's already overloaded. It does the 'who's free and not overworked' thinking for you, in under a minute."

---

## Slide 10 — Demo: Assigning a Substitute

**On screen instruction (live/recorded demo):**
- Open **Adjust Routine**, pick today's date
- Select the absent teacher
- Open a period's assignment sheet
- Show free vs busy, day counts, continuous stretch
- Assign a substitute and save

**Speaker note:**
> "Here it is live. I pick the teacher, open the assignment panel, and the system ranks substitutes — free teachers first, each with their load for the day and their consecutive periods. I pick one, assign, and save. The system re-verifies on the server that this substitute is genuinely free, so there's no double-booking. That's a completed substitution in seconds."

---

## Slide 11 — Dangerous Assignments? Controlled

**On screen bullets:**
- Ordinarily, a busy substitute is hard-blocked
- For intentionally heavy loads, a clear "Dangerous Assignment" confirmation appears
- The coordinator decides — with full visibility
- Nothing is ever double-booked, even on "save anyway"

**Speaker note:**
> "We understand real schools sometimes have no choice. So while a double-booking is always blocked, an intentionally heavy load can be saved after a clear warning. The coordinator makes the decision, but with full information — no silent surprises, and never a double-booked teacher."
